# SCYS Amazon Entry Manual — Normalized Knowledge

Source: `亚马逊入门｜实战手册｜2022年11月航海`

The authorized connector returned the full manual (~92.6k characters). This directory intentionally stores **derived knowledge only**, not a verbatim copy.

## Retained high-value areas

- Niche/market selection and demand decomposition.
- Competitor and customer-pain research.
- Product differentiation and iteration.
- Supplier evaluation, sampling, production and quality control.
- Listing positioning and conversion logic.
- Inventory, logistics, lead-time and cash-efficiency thinking.
- Unit economics and profit decomposition.
- Post-sale feedback loops and return/quality analysis.

## Time-sensitive areas marked for revalidation

- Registration procedures and account-verification details.
- Historical fees and FBA/logistics assumptions.
- Historical advertising heuristics and absolute ACOS/CPC thresholds.
- Tool/vendor recommendations.
- Any platform algorithm claims stated as deterministic rules.

## Blocked areas

Legacy tactics involving fake reviews/orders, incentivized reviews, manipulation of ranking/reviews, or account-risk evasion are retained only as negative examples in `policies/blocked_legacy_tactics.yaml` and must never be executable.
